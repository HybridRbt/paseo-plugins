Quick prompts turns the messages you send often into pills above every agent's composer. Tap a pill and its text goes to that agent as your next message, the same as typing it and pressing send.

Use it for prompts you repeat across agents, such as "Review the uncommitted changes", "Run the tests and fix failures", or "Summarize what changed and open a PR".

## Settings

Open **Settings → Plugins → paseo-quick-prompts → Quick prompts** to add, edit, reorder, and delete prompts. Each one has a label, shown on the pill, and the prompt text that is sent. Changes apply after you press **Save**; **Discard** returns to the saved list.

- Up to 20 prompts. Labels can be up to 40 characters.
- Pills appear in the order of the list, on every agent that belongs to a workspace.
- With no prompts saved, each agent shows an **Add quick prompt** pill that opens the settings page.

Prompts are stored on the Paseo host and shared by every app connected to it.

## Limits

- A pill sends right away. It does not put the text in the composer for editing first.
- Pills appear on existing agents, not in the composer for a new agent.
